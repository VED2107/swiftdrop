Questions

Do I need to install anything on my phone?

No. SwiftDrop opens in Safari or Chrome on your phone after you scan the code. Only the PC runs the app.

Does it work with Android?

Yes. iPhone and Android both work: scan with the camera (or Google Lens), or type the six-character code shown on the PC.

Can two phones send to each other without a PC?

Yes, as a preview. Open phone to phone on both, and each scans the other's code once. Files go directly between the phones; the codes only carry connection details.

Do I need internet?

No. Both devices only need to be on the same Wi-Fi. With no Wi-Fi around, turn on your phone's hotspot and connect the PC to it.

Windows asked about the firewall. Is that normal?

Yes. Allow SwiftDrop on private networks so your phone can reach your PC. If you install for all users, the installer sets this up for you.

Can I rename my phone, or pair it again?

Yes. Rename it on the PC in Settings, or from the phone itself. Pairing the same phone again keeps one entry instead of adding a second.

Are there size limits?

Not in practice. Big videos go straight to disk on the PC. Keep SwiftDrop on screen on your phone while it sends, since phones pause web pages in the background.

Will my iPhone photos stay HEIC?

The photo picker may convert them to JPEG. For originals, tap Options, then Current, in the picker, or send them from the Files app.
