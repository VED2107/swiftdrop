Questions

Do I need to install anything on my iPhone?

No. SwiftDrop on your PC or Android shows a code; your iPhone scans it and runs SwiftDrop in Safari.

How do the PC and Android connect?

Install SwiftDrop on both, on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Scan one's code with the other, or type its address. They talk to each other directly.

Do I need internet?

No. Both devices only need the same Wi-Fi. With no Wi-Fi around, turn on a phone's hotspot and join it from the other device.

Why does the iPhone take a moment before big videos start?

iOS copies what you pick out of the Photos library before handing it to Safari, and downloads it first if it lives in iCloud. If the picker converts videos, tap Options at the top of the picker and choose Current to keep originals and skip the conversion.

Windows asked about the firewall. Is that normal?

Yes. Allow SwiftDrop on private networks so your phone can reach your PC.

Are there size limits?

Not in practice with a PC: big videos go straight to disk. Keep SwiftDrop on screen while it sends, since phones pause web pages in the background.
